Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

NVT Dashboard is a private, personal tool created and used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

After the owner's explicit authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the app reads only the owner's own profile information (display name, avatar) and statistics (followers, likes, comments, views and video metrics) through the official TikTok API.

How data is used

This data is used only to display personal charts and live counters to the owner. No data from any other TikTok user is collected.

Storage and sharing

Statistics may be stored privately to build history charts. Data is never sold, shared with third parties or published.

Deletion and revocation

The owner can delete stored data at any time and can revoke the app's access from the TikTok app settings.
